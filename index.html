The problem

Humans can read them. Machines often cannot use them reliably.

Books, research articles, scanned records and archival documents contain valuable Arabic knowledge. OCR errors, reading-order failures and layout loss make that knowledge harder to search, structure and use in modern AI systems.

01

Arabic-first

Designed around Arabic script, document conditions and failure modes from the beginning.

02

Document-aware

The direction goes beyond plain text toward layout, reading order, pages and structured document outputs.

03

Evidence-driven

Model selection is benchmark-led, using reproducible metrics and stored outputs rather than selected screenshots.

Data strategy

Data can scale faster than storage.

Clouda stores what is expensive or impossible to replace, while deterministic synthetic variants can be regenerated from source text and seeds.

Store what matters.

Restricted, private, hard-to-reacquire and expensive-to-prepare assets get the highest retention and backup priority.

Raw source→Prepared data→Priority backup

Regenerate what can be regenerated.

Arabic text corpora can be rendered into clean PDFs, page images and degraded scans on demand using reproducible seeds.

Text→Render→Degrade
