About & Contact

Independent, open-source Arabic OCR & Document AI infrastructure.

Clouda OCR is focused on making Arabic documents more reliably searchable, structured and usable by software, researchers and modern AI systems.

Project

Open source at the core.

The core project is intended to remain fully open source. A paid hosted service may later serve users who prefer managed convenience or high-volume use, but that hosted service does not exist yet.

Operating principles
01

Evidence before claims

Public benchmark results and technical claims should be backed by reproducible artifacts whenever possible.

02

Respect data permissions

Public, restricted and training-only data are treated differently. Not all project data can or should be redistributed.

03

Open infrastructure

The long-term goal is Arabic document tooling that researchers and developers can inspect, adapt and build upon.
