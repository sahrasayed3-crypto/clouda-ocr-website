About

One project. One name. Every claim traceable.

Clouda OCR is an independent, open-source, Arabic-first document-processing and OCR infrastructure project — an answer to a concrete gap, built as a stack, and measured in public.

What is Clouda OCR?

An Arabic-first, open-source document-processing and OCR infrastructure project for turning PDFs and scanned documents into reliable, editable, machine-usable outputs. The current runtime handles trusted born-digital text and document routing; scanned-page OCR remains gated behind validated model integration while model evaluation and data preparation continue. It is built first for Arabic-heavy archives, digitization teams, researchers, publishers, and document-processing teams that need editable, searchable output from difficult PDFs and scans while keeping control over their data and infrastructure.

Why does Clouda OCR exist?

Arabic documents — books, records, archives — are full of knowledge that machines cannot reliably use: OCR errors, broken reading order, lost page context. Generic pipelines silently fail on RTL text and degraded scans. Clouda OCR takes the opposite approach: make every capability explicit, measure models independently, and never present an unverified guess as document text. The project is self-hostable by design, so teams keep control of their documents and infrastructure.

What exists today?

Verified

  • Open-source runtime — released as v0.2.0 (Apache-2.0): trusted born-digital PDF-to-DOCX conversion, page-level trust gate, honest routing states, RTL-aware output.
  • Published Arabic OCR benchmark — v0.1.0: 177 distorted Arabic pages, 6 full-coverage models, separate DOI (benchmark page).
  • Data + evaluation infrastructure — deterministic generation/degradation, provenance and licensing classification, reproducible scoring.
  • Not yet — the final trained OCR model and any hosted API/product. No paying customers or pilots are claimed; there is no traction to report yet, and the site does not dress that up.
Why is Clouda OCR model-agnostic?

Strong OCR models already exist, and Clouda OCR does not pretend otherwise. The runtime's extraction interface and engine registry make the OCR engine a replaceable component: candidate models are measured on the Arabic benchmark, the strongest suitable open, self-hostable model gets integrated, and adaptation or training happens only if the evidence demonstrates a persistent gap. If existing models solve the problem, Clouda OCR uses them.

What is the open-source strategy?

Apache-2.0

Open source is the distribution model; the intended advantage is execution: Arabic-specific evaluation methodology, provenance-controlled data pipelines, benchmark history and reproducibility, document-level routing and quality gates, RTL-aware reconstruction behavior, accumulated failure cases and evaluation evidence, and integration know-how. No proprietary datasets, patents, or network effects are claimed — the moat, if it develops, is the quality of the evaluation, the data discipline, and the document pipeline, proven in public.

Evidence before claims
