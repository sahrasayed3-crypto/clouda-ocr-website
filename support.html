Support & Partnerships

Support the next technical milestone, not a vague future promise.

No project-trained production OCR model yet. The software and research foundation is largely in place. A 177-page Arabic OCR benchmark has been completed and a leading candidate model has been identified for further validation. Newer training components are currently awaiting final integration review. Real model adaptation and training still require hardware validation.

Two primary support channels

Financial and infrastructure support are tracked separately. Any contribution through either channel is useful.

Financial Support

Direct cash support for the current phase. The current cash target is $15,000. This is non-equity, open-source project support. There is no minimum contribution, no requirement from a single supporter, and multiple payments from multiple supporters are expected. Any amount is useful.

This target does not include infrastructure, cloud, API, hardware, or compute support.

Why local compute is needed

Cloud remains useful for temporary bursts that exceed local capacity, but it does not replace local hardware for real validation and iteration.

  • Repeatability and persistent access to an owned environment
  • Restricted-data handling under full project control
  • Lower iteration friction without vendor scheduling or billing uncertainty
  • Real VRAM measurement and BF16 / FP16 mixed-precision validation
  • Throughput and batch-size measurements on real hardware
  • Checkpoint / resume behavior testing
  • Real model adaptation and pilot training
