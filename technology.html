Reproducibility

Evidence is part of the benchmark, not an afterthought.

The public package includes methodology, manifests, model/config provenance, public audit records and SHA-256 checksums.

01

Canonical manifest

177 unique sample IDs define the benchmark registry and prevent silent subset drift.

02

Canonical evaluator

Normalization and metric code are published so CER/WER scoring can be reproduced consistently.

03

Public audit

Sanitization, license review, checksums and consistency artifacts are preserved in the release.
